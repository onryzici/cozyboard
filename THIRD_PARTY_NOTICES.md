## Original project art and adaptation

Keyboard meshes, shaders, supply boxes and gameplay scripts are project-authored. The installation timing (align, press, resistance, click, settle) is adapted from the user's LittleSwitch project. Only its sounds, music and interaction timing were reused, not its workshop asset packs.

Painted interface images were generated using the built-in image generation tool; paths, prompts and source dimensions are documented in `Documentation/GeneratedArt.md`. Reference screenshots supplied in chat are not included in this repository.
